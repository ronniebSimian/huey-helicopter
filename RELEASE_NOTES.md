**For Minecraft 1.21.11 (Fabric).** Requires [Fabric API](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api).

## 0.1.1
- **Door-gun overheating works as intended:** holding the trigger overheats the M60 in about 4 seconds (29 rounds), then locks it for about 4 seconds. Fire in bursts! (Before, it took about 30 seconds of non-stop fire.)
- The heat gauge stays red for the whole lockout.
- Players still on 0.1.0 can join a 0.1.1 host, but everyone should update to stay matched.

Install the jar on the host/server **and** every player's client. See the [guide](https://github.com/ronniebSimian/huey-helicopter/blob/main/GUIDE.md).
