**For Minecraft 1.21.11 (Fabric).** Requires [Fabric API](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api).

Install the jar on the server **and** on every player's client. See the [README](https://github.com/ronniebSimian/huey-helicopter#installing) for step-by-step instructions.

- Flyable UH-1 Huey with rotor spool-up, hover, and banking turns
- 8 seats: pilot, copilot, 2 M60 door gunners, 4 troops (press **G** to switch seats)
- Door guns with tracers and overheating
- Hull damage, repair with iron ingots, craftable in survival
